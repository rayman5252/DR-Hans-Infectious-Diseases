Ep. 011 - 12,000 Years of Care: Lessons from Romito Cave

Rare diseases are often framed as modern medical mysteries, but the story of Romito Cave in Italy reminds us that they have been part of the human experience for millennia. On this Rare Disease Day, we honor the 300 million people worldwide living with a rare condition.
