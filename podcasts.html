Podcast Episodes

Meet Dr. Hans - An Introduction

Hello! I'm a rare disease Dad and doctor who can't help himself to talk about rare diseases. Hopefully I touch upon a topic of your interest but please feel free to drop me a line.

Ep. 001 - Starting Down the Path

A touching insight into the origin of Dr. Hans' interest in rare diseases and how it touches his family.

Ep. 002 - Covering Goals

In covering goals I have more on my son and Syngap1. Finding a balance between managing expectations and aiming for a cure! Hope you enjoy the listen.

Ep. 003 - ICD-10

Podcast #3 takes a trip down memory lane with ICD-10. Keep fighting for your code! You deserve it! Hope you enjoy the listen and please feel free to reach out!

Ep. 004 - Today it's ICD-11!

ICD-11 will be the worldwide standard in coming years so get in now — it'll be easier than you think! While Syngap is indexed to LD90.Y, we have a permanent Unique Identifier here: id.who.int/icd/entity/1830660574

Ep. 005 - Transition from Pediatric to Adult Care

At some point, you and the pediatrician need to plan transferring care of your 'little' loved one to a healthcare provider who manages adults. Talk about it, plan it, and passing your care from one provider to the next shouldn't let you down.

Ep. 006 - Learning Lessons from Saddest Outcomes

Today's podcast is about learning lessons from the saddest outcomes. As a rare disease Dad, physician, and teammate in drug development, I want to share my 2 cents in the hopes it helps.

Ep. 007 - The (Impressive) 2025 ASGCT

Today's podcast is about how impressive the ASGCT conference was this year! Beyond the huge news, there were also 4 abstracts on Syngap1. The Syngap1 space is heating up and has the First-in-Human milestone in sight!

Ep. 008 - Elevidys

Today's podcast is about the Elevidys rollercoaster of the past 2 weeks. Risk-benefit analysis is a bear the rare disease community, regulators, and the pharmaceutical industry constantly wrestle with. The best analysis happens when all the data is disclosed and discussed.

Ep. 009 - The Finish Line is Finally in View

On October 1st, Camp4 Therapeutics reached a pivotal milestone. In today's podcast, I walk you through the drug development process using Syngap1 as our lens. Stay hopeful. Stay realistic. And keep those fingers crossed.

Ep. 010 - Adverse Events Following Vaccines

Today's rare disease discussion focuses on severe adverse events following vaccination — a truly rare occurrence. Vaccination is ultimately an act of both personal protection and community care.
